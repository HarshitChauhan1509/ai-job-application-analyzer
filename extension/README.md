# CareerPilot — 1-Click Job Description Clipper (Chrome & Edge Extension)

Clip job descriptions directly from LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and YC Work at a Startup into your CareerPilot application pipeline with instant ATS match analysis.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Platform-Specific DOM Scraper**: Tailored scrapers for LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Greenhouse boards, Lever postings, and Ashby.
- **Instant ATS Match**: Automatically evaluates candidate fit against the primary CareerPilot resume in real time.
- **Stage Targeting**: Clip directly into `SAVED` (bookmarked) or `APPLIED` (active application) Kanban columns.
- **1-Click Deep Links**:
  - 🚀 **Open in Workspace**: Inspect extracted skills and application notes at `/applications/[id]`.
  - ✉️ **Draft Founder Pitch**: Pre-fills cold outreach pitch generator for that company.

---

## 🛠️ Installation Instructions (Takes 30 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome**, **Brave**, or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions` in Edge).
3. Toggle on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left toolbar.
5. Select the `extension/` folder in this repository:
   ```
   C:\Users\...\ai-job-application-analyzer\extension
   ```
6. The **CareerPilot Clipper** icon will now appear in your browser extension toolbar! Pin it for quick access.

---

## 💡 How to Use

1. Browse to any job posting on **LinkedIn**, **Indeed**, **Greenhouse**, or **Lever**.
2. Click the **CareerPilot** extension icon in your browser toolbar.
3. The job title, company name, location, and description will be auto-detected.
4. Select your target stage (`Saved` vs `Applied`).
5. Click **⚡ Clip & Analyze Fit**.
6. View your real-time **ATS Match Score** and click **Open in Workspace** or **Draft Founder Pitch**!
